Abundance as Freedom of Imagination

Kinavu expanded its offering with the launch of a dedicated kids collection, Earth Song. The new range called for a visual language that could feel connected to the parent brand while having its own distinct character. This led to the development of a separate logo, along with supporting collaterals and promotional material for the collection.

About the Collection

Hence the Collection is called

The collection features four prints, each rooted in a distinct story. Together, they explore how the unhurried rhythm of childhood nurtures imagination giving rise to curious questions and the quiet magic of make-believe worlds.

Earth Song

Each story imagines a playful, make-believe world shaped by nature, where fish take to the skies, birds drift through water, children float on clouds and trails of shooting stars, and leaves and plants come together to form homes.

A reflection of these imagined worlds where nature bends, shifts, and sings to the rhythm of childhood wonder.

Logo Development

Final Logo

Logo lockup without the main brand name

Logo lockup with the main brand name

The Earth Song logo draws from the idea of imagination as a continuous thread that connects the many worlds within the collection. The flowing line within and around the letterforms suggests a string in motion, one that wanders, loops and travels without following a fixed path. This becomes a thread of stories, reflecting the way a child’s imagination moves freely from one idea to another. Just as the prints imagine unexpected worlds where familiar elements can take on new roles, the thread connects these moments and allows one story to flow into the next.

The logo therefore becomes more than a name. Its flowing form represents the imaginative journey at the heart of Earth Song, where stories wander freely and the world is constantly reimagined.

Collaterals

Packaging Design

The packaging brings the world of Earth Song together through a single wandering thread. Emerging from and moving around the logo, the thread travels across the surface of the packaging, weaving its way between characters and elements from the collection’s prints. Fish, birds, mushrooms, stars and other imagined forms appear along its path, creating the feeling of a small world unfolding around the box. The thread gives the packaging a sense of movement and playfulness while also connecting the different stories within the collection. Rather than presenting the characters as separate decorative elements, they appear to be part of one continuous journey, as though each one has been discovered along the way.
