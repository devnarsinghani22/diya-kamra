Hi, I’m Diya.

Graphic & Communication DesignerNational Institute of Design (NID), Bachelor in Design (Communication Design), 2026

‍

I’m a visual communication student who likes thinking through design, type, and imagery. I enjoy working with ideas that come from observation and everyday moments, and turning them into visuals that feel thoughtful and playful.

‍

My work often moves across posters, identities, mixed media, and small experiments. I’m interested in how typography, form, and composition can quietly tell stories or communicate a feeling without saying too much. I like exploring symbols, textures, and details, and figuring out how they come together to create meaning.

‍

Outside of work, I’m usually collecting references, noticing things around me, or thinking about ideas that might turn into something later.
