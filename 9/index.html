Diya Kamra

The project focused on strengthening and evolving the visual and communication language of the brand, with the aim of creating a more cohesive, distinctive, and emotionally resonant brand.


The central objective was to translate the brand’s strongly defined values into a distinctive visual language that extended beyond the logo, creating a cohesive identity with both emotional resonance and strong recall value.


Through the development of visual assets, imagery, and communication, the project aimed to explore how intangible ideas and feelings could be transformed into a recognisable and meaningful brand experience.


As the brand enhances its market presence, it was important to form a narrative that could capture and communicate the emotions Kinavu seeks to evoke in both its audience and investors.



Kinavu Living is an Indian direct-to-consumer home-bedding brand operating primarily through its e-commerce website and Instagram presence.


Kinavu approaches bedding as more than a functional product, recognising the bedroom as a personal space for rest, work, intimacy, solitude, family, and dreaming. The brand aims to create bedding that is thoughtful, breathable and beautiful, while building a deeper connection between the products and the lives lived around them.
